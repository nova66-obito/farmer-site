business ideas

animals farms

raising animals

Poultry farming: Raising chickens, ducks, turkeys, and geese for meat and eggs. This is a popular and profitable business that can be started with a small investment.

Beekeeping: Maintaining bee colonies to produce honey, beeswax, and other products. This is a sustainable business with a high profit margin.

Pig farming: Raising pigs for meat.

Rabbit farming: Raising rabbits for meat.

Aquaculture

Marine aquaculture: Farming species that live in the ocean and estuaries, such as oysters, clams, mussels, shrimp, and seaweeds

Freshwater aquaculture: Farming species in ponds or other manmade systems, such as catfish and trout
